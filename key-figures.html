Key Figures in Paris’s Colonial Art Networks

The individuals featured here are the main protagonists of the provenance stories on this site. Together, they formed part of the extensive colonial network that shaped, sustained, and financed the Paris art world. Their names appear in green throughout the site’s interactive provenance dashboards, where you can trace their relationships with artworks, places, and each other.

Most of these figures were collectors, patrons, artists, dealers, or members of elite families whose lives and careers were closely entwined with France’s colonial empire. Their investments and commercial activities in transatlantic trade and plantation economies, along with their family connections and social networks, reveal the entanglements with colonialism and enslavement that structured the production, circulation, and collecting of art in this period.

While the provenance stories in the Gallery trace the journeys of artworks, these biographies uncover the people whose actions, decisions, and relationships determined those journeys.
